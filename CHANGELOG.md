# Changelog

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-14

- Clarified the distinction between the QMF foundation and the paper's augmentation contribution.
- Corrected the full name of AW to Adversarial Uncertainty Weight.
- Added support for modality-specific energy temperatures in QMF.
- Preserved mixed train/eval states when generating adversarial low-quality inputs.
- Added validation for adversarial-generation hyperparameters.
- Expanded tests to cover modality-specific temperatures, ranking direction, and model-state preservation.
- Clarified that examples use synthetic vectors and toy encoders rather than real dataset pipelines.
- Added a faculty reading guide and a personal contribution statement template.
- Strengthened license and provenance language; no open-source license is asserted.
